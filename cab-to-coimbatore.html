Where the car drops in Coimbatore

Hotels, house addresses, Gandhipuram, the railway stations and Coimbatore International Airport can all be the last stop. A pin in WhatsApp is better than a neighbourhood name. If you have a flight, leave more than the five-hour clear-road estimate. Traffic at either end, and a tea stop, belong in the plan.

One way or round trip

A one-way drop from Theni to Coimbatore is a normal booking. A same-day return is about ten hours of driving plus whatever you do in the city. That is a hard day for one driver. If you need the car to wait, or to stay for a night and return the next day, say that in the first message so the quote includes it.

Pickup in Coimbatore back to Theni is the same road when the date is open. Periyakulam pickup is also fine; it sits about 15 km from Theni, so it barely changes a five-hour journey.

What to expect in the car

Four passengers, air conditioning, and space for bags in the boot or on the carrier. It is a sedan, not a van. If you are more than four, this is the wrong vehicle and we will say so rather than squeeze the trip.
