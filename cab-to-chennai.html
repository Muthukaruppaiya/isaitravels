How the day is planned

From Theni the usual road goes by Batlagundu and Dindigul, through Tiruchirappalli, then on toward Chennai. Perambalur is a sensible stretch for a proper break. This is not a trip to start late and rush. Tell us if anyone needs a longer food stop, or if a child or an older passenger should not sit for more than a few hours at a time.

One way, or two days

Book Chennai as a one-way drop, or as a round trip with a night halt. A same-day up-and-down is more than a thousand kilometres. Isai Cabs & Travels will not treat that as a normal booking. There is one driver, and the car goes back to Theni only on a plan that leaves room to drive safely.

Airport, stations and night starts

Chennai International Airport and the main railway stations can be the pickup or the drop. City traffic changes the last hour, so send the flight or train time, not only the neighbourhood. A night departure is possible. The quote then follows that clock, including where the breaks fall.

Four passengers is the comfortable limit. Use the roof carrier for large bags. For a shorter city run, the Coimbatore cab is a different kind of day.
