Periyakulam to Theni

The hop is about 15 km and often takes around half an hour. People book it for the Theni bus stand, a hospital visit, a function, or to join a longer ride that starts in Theni. Send the street or a shop name. “Periyakulam town” is too wide for a single car to search.

Periyakulam to Kodaikanal

The ghat begins after Periyakulam. From Theni the hill station is about 85 km and a little over two hours; from Periyakulam the road is shorter, and the slow part is still the climb. The Swift Dzire is a normal sedan, happy on this road if we are not in a hurry. If someone in the group prefers extra stops on the bends, say so when you ask for the fare.

A day trip can include waiting in Kodaikanal. That waiting time is part of the quote. The car stays with you only for the hours you booked.

Further from Periyakulam

The same driver continues to Madurai, Palani, Coimbatore and Chennai. For a flight, add buffer beyond the usual drive. Coimbatore is about 211 km from Theni, so a Periyakulam start changes the clock only a little. Chennai remains a full-day one-way trip of about 504 km.
